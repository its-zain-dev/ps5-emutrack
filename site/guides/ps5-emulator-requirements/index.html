PS5 Emulator System Requirements: CPU, GPU (Vulkan), RAM & OS Guide

Upstream PS5 emulator PC specs for KytyPS5 & Prosperity: x86-64 AVX/SSE4.2 CPUs, Vulkan 1.3/1.4 GPUs, 16-32 GB RAM, and Windows vs. Linux support.

Minimum vs. realistic desktop requirements

Unlike console ports optimized for PC, PS5 emulators translate RDNA 2 shaders to Vulkan SPIR-V on the fly and allocate host memory directly for unified PS5 RAM pools. Based on the official upstream documentation from KytyPS5 and Prosperity, here is what you actually need:

  • Operating System: Windows 10 (1803+) / Windows 11 x64, or a modern 64-bit Linux distribution (Ubuntu, Arch, CachyOS, NixOS). macOS works experimentally on Apple Silicon via Rosetta 2 + MoltenVK in KytyPS5, or via dedicated macOS ports.
  • CPU: Modern 64-bit x86-64 processor (made in the last ~10 years) supporting AVX, SSE4.2, and BMI1. On ARM64 hosts (Apple Silicon or Android), CPU translation layers such as Rosetta 2 or FEX are required.
  • GPU & Vulkan: A dedicated GPU with current drivers supporting Vulkan 1.3 (required by KytyPS5) or Vulkan 1.4+ (specified by Prosperity), plus Buffer Device Address (BDA) support.
  • RAM & VRAM: Expect 16 GB–32 GB of system RAM and at least 8 GB of dedicated VRAM (the PS5 shares 16 GB GDDR6 across CPU and GPU, so 6 GB cards frequently hit allocation failures on heavy AAA dumps).

What real compatibility logs tell us about hardware

In our verified upstream reports, testers running desktop GPUs like the RTX 3080 Ti, RTX 4070 SUPER, RX 6800 XT, and RTX 5070 Ti with 32 GB DDR4/DDR5 still encounter shader compilation crashes or descriptor assertion errors. Right now, compatibility bottlenecks are mostly unimplemented OS syscalls and RDNA 2 shader edge cases rather than raw GPU speed.
