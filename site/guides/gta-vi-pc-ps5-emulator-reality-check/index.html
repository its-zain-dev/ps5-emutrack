GTA IV vs. GTA V vs. GTA VI: Roman numeral & platform disambiguation

Searchers looking for GTA VI news frequently run into Roman-numeral confusion with GTA IV (4) or older PS5 ports:

  • GTA IV (Grand Theft Auto IV, 2008): A PS3, Xbox 360, and PC release—not a PS5 title. GTA IV has an official PC edition and is outside the scope of PS5 emulators tracked here.
  • GTA V (Grand Theft Auto V): Released across PS3, PS4, PC, and PS5. As of November 2026, KytyPS5 reaches verified In-Game gameplay on PS5 GTA V (with cutscene crashes and graphical bugs), providing an important RAGE-engine benchmark.
  • GTA VI (Grand Theft Auto VI / GTA6): Officially released on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S on November 19, 2026, with no PC version announced yet. Follow verified progress via our GTA VI tracker and verify downloads with our anti-scam guide.
