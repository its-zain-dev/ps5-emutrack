Can an Android phone or iPad run PS5 games today?

Not meaningfully for commercial AAA games. Desktop x86-64 PCs with 32 GB RAM and desktop Vulkan GPUs are still in early bring-up stages. On Android ARM64 smartphones and iOS/iPadOS tablets, an emulator has to perform extra x86-64-to-ARM64 CPU translation (using JITs like FEXCore or Box64) on top of RDNA 2-to-Vulkan/Metal shader translation, within tight mobile thermal and memory limits (typically 12+ GB RAM and Vulkan 1.4+ or Metal drivers recommended).

What open-source mobile projects actually exist?

You can inspect every tracked mobile port in our Ports & Frontends directory:

  • Android experimental ports: KytyPS5-Android (FEXCore + Mesa Turnip), SharpDroid, SharpEmu Android, NovaPS5 Android, and Lumen Emu.
  • iOS & iPadOS experimental ports: Metaspero (Apple Silicon dynamic translation), madeira-anyps5 (AnyPS5 + Madeira Wine ARM64EC + FEX + MoltenVK on iPad), MagnusPS5, and AetherPS4 (PS4 emulator core for iOS/iPadOS).

Treat all mobile PS5 ports as experimental research rather than daily gaming apps.
