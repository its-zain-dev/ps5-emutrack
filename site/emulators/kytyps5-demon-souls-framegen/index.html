Release Notes

What's new in v1.3: based on the new upstream KytyPS5 v20261014 by chenxiao07, with all of this build's improvements kept.

  • ⭐ New launcher layout: the settings are in tabs (Display, Graphics, Performance, System) under the game folder; the window remembers the last tab.
  • ⭐ Shrine of Storms freeze work (known issue #1): large textures now upload in 64 MB pieces and the partial path no longer re-uploads the whole 1.8 GB pool; this build also turns on upstream's layer-range textures, so the pool's views become small textures of the layers they read (upstream issue #9). Not confirmed at the spot yet: known issue #1 stays open until a session shows it.
  • About 2 GB less RAM after a save loads, and no more memory growth when travelling between worlds.
  • HDR and VSync options (Display tab, off by default). With Frame Generation, a G-SYNC display is the better choice than VSync.
  • Fixes from upstream: a crash when travelling to the Tower of Latria by archstone, a 1.05 crash in Boletaria 1-1, very large textures on 8 GB GPUs, game folders with very long paths.
  • More precompile hints: shaders from tours of every map, so more first visits are smooth after precompiling.
  • Your shader cache is kept: upstream now caps the driver shader cache at 1/64 of RAM; this build allows 1/16 (2 GB with 32 GB RAM), so a typical 1.2 GB cache is not thrown away at every launch.

Everything from v1.2 stays: DLSS Frame Generation (requires an NVIDIA RTX 40 or 50 series card), render resolution upscaling (the game's own upscaler, not DLSS/FSR), ultrawide for any ultrawide screen, the voices fix.

Updating from v1.2: extract the new zip over your old folder (your saves, launcher settings and shader caches stay; the frame generation camera list is replaced by the one in the zip). Your precompiled shaders stay valid; run "Precompile shaders" again if you want the new map-tour shaders too.

Downloads: KytyPS5-DemonsSouls-FrameGen.zip is the build. KytyPS5-DemonsSouls-FrameGen-source.zip is its complete source (KytyPS5 is GPL 2.0; third-party licenses in THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES.md).

No game files included. Unofficial build, not affiliated with Sony, NVIDIA or the KytyPS5 developers.
